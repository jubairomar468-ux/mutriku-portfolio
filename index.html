About me

I’m Jubair Ibn Omar Hamza, an MSc Renewable Energy Engineering student at the University of Exeter. My background combines electrical and electronic engineering with postgraduate study in data science.

I have worked in university teaching and simulation-based research, with experience spanning physiological signal analysis, device modelling and power-system studies. I work with MATLAB, Python and COMSOL to connect physical models with measured data.
